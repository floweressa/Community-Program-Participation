Features

Everything your community needs

One platform for program management, participant tracking, attendance monitoring, and in-depth analytics — built for real community work.

Program Management

Create and manage community programs with detailed info on location, schedule, and status — ongoing, upcoming, or completed.

Participant Tracking

Register participants, manage their profiles, and monitor their involvement across multiple community programs in one place.

Attendance Monitoring

Log and review daily attendance per session. Track present and absent records, and see rates at a glance across all programs.

Analytics & Reports

Platform-wide KPIs, monthly attendance breakdowns, top participant rankings, and program comparisons — all exportable.

Role-Based Access

Separate dashboards and permissions for Admins, Staff, and Participants — every user sees exactly what they need.

Data Export

Export participant lists, attendance sheets, and reports to CSV or PDF — perfect for official documentation and filing.

How It Works

Up and running in minutes

From registration to real-time attendance — the full workflow is straightforward for staff and participants alike.

1
Create an account

Participants register with their email and basic profile. Staff accounts are provisioned by the administrator from the admin portal.

2
Browse & join programs

Participants discover available community programs and register for the ones that match their interests and schedule.

3
Staff logs attendance

Staff members mark attendance each session. Present and absent records are saved and immediately reflected in analytics.

4
Track progress & export

Everyone monitors their participation history. Staff and admins access full analytics and can export reports at any time.
